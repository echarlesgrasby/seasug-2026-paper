## Eric Grasby Bio

Eric Charles Grasby is an experienced programmer analyst with professional experience spanning the database marketing and electric regional transmission organization (RTO) sectors. 
He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Information Science and a Master of Science in Information Quality from the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, 
where he is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer and Information Science. 
His research interests focus on the intersection of language engineering and information quality. 
Since 2020, he has worked at Southwest Power Pool, most recently serving in the organization’s Market Monitoring Unit since 2023.
